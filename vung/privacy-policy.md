# Privacy Policy

**App:** Vung (the "App") · **Last updated:** 9 November 2026 · **Effective from:** 15 November 2026

[Tiếng Việt](../vi/privacy/) · [Suomi](../fi/privacy/) · [Svenska](../sv/privacy/) · [Deutsch](../de/privacy/)

## 1. Summary

- **Your financial data stays on your device.** Everything you enter (income, spending, debts, assets, goals, score) is stored only on your phone. We do not receive it and we cannot see it.
- **No account.** You do not need to sign up or log in. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
- **A small server for invites and free Plus, nothing else.** If you use **Invite friends**, or if our support gives you free Plus, our server stores a random ID for the App, your invite code and the rewards. It never receives your financial data (see section 4.2).
- **The free version shows ads. Plus has none.** Ads are provided by Google AdMob and involve some data leaving your device (see section 4.4). We never give advertisers your debts, income, assets or score.
- **We never sell your personal data**, and we do not track you across other companies' apps and websites.
- **You can delete everything** at any time, in the App and by emailing us (see section 9).

## 3. Data you enter stays on your device

When you set up and use the App you may enter: country, language, currency, age, gender (optional), occupation, whether your income is stable, number of dependents, monthly income and spending, savings and investment amounts, debts (balances, interest rates, payment and extra-payment amounts, payment days), assets, savings goals, big expenses, and your notification preferences.

- This data is stored in a database inside the App's private storage on your device.
- We do **not** collect it. It is not sent to us or to any third party, and it is not used for advertising.
- Scores, charts, estimates and tips are calculated on your device.
- Reminders and goal celebrations are **local notifications** created by your phone. We do not run a push-notification server.
- Your phone's own backup (for example iCloud Backup or a computer backup) may include the App's data, depending on your device settings. That backup is controlled by Apple or you, not by us.

Because the data stays on your device, **we cannot recover it** if you delete the App, reset or lose your phone and have no device backup.

## 4. Data that does leave your device

### 4.1 Subscription and purchase data (Vung Plus)
Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store (or by Google on Android). We do not receive your payment card or Apple ID details. To confirm whether Plus is active, the App uses RevenueCat, which receives the App's random ID (see 4.2), your purchase and subscription status, the App version, device model and operating system, your App Store country, and your IP address. RevenueCat acts as our processor.

### 4.2 Support ID, invites and free Plus (our server)
**Support ID.** When you first open the App, it creates a random ID (for example `vung_3f9a…`). It is not derived from your name, phone number, Apple ID or any hardware identifier. You can see it in **Profile → Support ID**. The same ID is used with RevenueCat (4.1).

**Invite friends.** When you open **Invite friends**, the App sends your support ID to our server, which creates your invite code. When someone enters a code, their App sends:
- their support ID and the code;
- **Apple App Attest** data: a new cryptographic key created in their phone's Secure Enclave, and Apple's confirmation that the request comes from a genuine copy of the App on a real Apple device. It contains no name, phone number, Apple ID, advertising ID or serial number. We use it only to stop fake or automated invites, and we keep the key's identifier so the same key cannot be used twice.

Our server then stores: both support IDs, the invite code, which ID invited which, the status, the free days granted and when. It asks RevenueCat to add the free Plus time to the inviter's ID (it sends the support ID and the end date).

**Free Plus from us.** If you contact support and send us your support ID, we may give you free Plus or lifetime Plus. We then store your support ID, the type and length of the grant, a short reason (for example "support request"), when it was given and the database account that recorded it. The grant reaches the App through RevenueCat.

**What the server never receives:** your financial data, anything you entered in the App, your contacts, your location or your advertising ID.

**Server logs.** Like any internet service, our server and its hosting provider see your IP address and the time of each request. We use this only for security and to prevent abuse, and keep it for up to 30 days.

**Hosting.** The server and its database run with Heroku (Salesforce, Inc.), in its EU region (data centres of Amazon Web Services in Ireland). The provider acts as our processor.

### 4.3 Interest-rate data
If you track a mortgage linked to Euribor, the App may request the latest reference rate from a public data service (the European Central Bank's data portal). Like any internet request, this reveals your IP address and device type to that service. The request contains none of your financial data.

### 4.4 Advertising (free version only)
The free version shows ads labelled "Sponsored", provided by **Google AdMob** (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). While an ad is requested and shown, Google may process your IP address (from which an approximate location is derived), device and operating-system information, an app-specific or device identifier, and information about how you interact with ads. Google may use this for ad delivery, frequency limits, measurement, fraud prevention and security.

What we do to limit this:
- We request **non-personalised ads only**. Ads are chosen from general context (your country, language and which screen you are on), not from a profile of you.
- We **never** pass your debts, income, assets, score or other entered data to Google or advertisers.
- We do not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, because we do not do that.
- We do not show ads in setup, forms, the score screen or the paywall, and we show at most one ad per screen.
- In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and other regions where the law requires it, the App shows **Google's consent message** before ads are requested, and Google's measurement starts only after that. Ads are served only according to your choice; if you decline, you may see fewer ads or none. You can reopen the choices at any time in **Profile → Privacy and ads choices**.
- **Vung Plus turns off all ads**, and no ad requests are made while Plus is active.

Google's own handling of this data is described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

### 4.5 Information you send us
If you email us for support, we receive your email address, what you write, and your support ID if you include it. Please do not send financial details you do not want us to see.

### 4.6 Technical reports from Apple
If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated crash and usage reports. We cannot identify you from them.

### 4.7 What we do not do
We do not use analytics or advertising-profiling software of our own, we do not use fingerprinting, we do not build a profile of you, we do not buy data about you, and we do not sell, rent or trade personal data.

## 8. How long we keep data

- **Data on your device:** until you delete it or the App.
- **Invite records and invite codes on our server:** while the invite programme runs and up to 24 months after your last invite activity, then deleted.
- **App Attest key IDs:** as long as the invite record they belong to. Unused one-time challenges are deleted within one hour.
- **Free Plus grants:** while the grant is active and up to 24 months after it ends, as a record of what we gave.
- **Server logs (IP address, request time):** up to 30 days.
- **Subscription records (RevenueCat):** while your subscription relationship exists, and after that for as long as needed for accounting and legal claims. In Finland, accounting records are generally kept for six years.
- **Support emails:** up to 12 months after the matter is closed.
- **Advertising data at Google:** according to Google's policies.

## 9. Your choices and rights

**In the App (Profile):**
- **Delete all data:** erases everything the App stores on your device and resets the App, which then creates a new support ID. This cannot be undone. It does **not** delete records on our server or at RevenueCat (see below).
- **Privacy and ads choices:** review or change your ad choices (shown where consent is required).
- **Reminders:** change the time, or hide amounts on the lock screen.
- **Export** (Plus): export your data as a file.
- **Support ID:** see or share the ID that our server knows you by.

**Delete or see your data on our server:** email us your support ID (before using "Delete all data", if you can) and tell us what you want. We delete or send the invite records, codes and grants linked to it within one month. A free Plus grant ends when we delete it.

**On your phone:** delete the App; reset your device's advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in iOS Settings; manage or cancel your subscription in Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

**Legal rights.** If the GDPR applies to you, you have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing of personal data we hold about you, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time (withdrawal does not affect earlier processing). Our server knows you only by your random support ID, so we need that ID to find your data. To exercise rights relating to data processed by Google, Apple or RevenueCat you can also contact them directly.

Email us at tikki.dinh@gmail.com. We reply within one month.

**Complaints.** You may complain to the supervisory authority in your country of residence or work. In Finland this is the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman (Tietosuojavaltuutetun toimisto, tietosuoja.fi). In Sweden it is IMY (imy.se), in Germany the data protection authority of your federal state.

## 10. Security

- **On your device:** your data is protected by the App's private storage, the iOS or Android sandbox, and your device's lock screen and encryption. **The App does not add its own encryption on top of this**, so we strongly recommend a device passcode or biometric lock.
- **On our server:** the App talks only to our server's API over encrypted HTTPS, and never directly to the database. The database accepts only encrypted connections with credentials that only we hold, and the App has no such credentials. Only we can open it, through our hosting account protected by two-factor authentication. Grants cannot be changed after they are recorded, and every grant and every revocation is logged with its time. Keys for RevenueCat stay on the server and are never in the App.
- All connections from the App to the services described above use encrypted HTTPS.
