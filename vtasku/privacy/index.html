Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-15

This policy explains what vTasku (“the app”) does with information. The short version: your expenses, notes, receipt photos and budgets stay on your device. We have no user accounts and we cannot read them.

1. Who is responsible

vTasku is operated by Thinh Dinh, Finland. Contact for privacy questions: tikki.dinh@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your device

All expenses, categories, notes, receipt photos, budgets, recurring expenses, searches and settings are stored only on your device. vTasku does not connect to your bank or wallet. The Apple Pay capture feature only receives the amount and merchant name that you set up in an Apple Shortcut, and keeps them on your device.

3. What leaves your device

FeatureWhat is sentTo whom
Ads (Free plan)Device identifiers, IP address (approximate location), ad interactions, diagnosticsGoogle AdMob
Plus purchaseAn anonymous user ID and your purchase historyRevenueCat, with Apple App Store / Google Play
Invite codes (when available)A random ID made on your device, your invite code, a code you enter, and an Apple App Attest key (see section 7)Our server
iCloud backup (Plus, optional)A copy of your expenses, budgets, labels and recurring itemsYour own iCloud account, stored by Apple

The iCloud backup is off until you turn it on in Settings. Apple stores the copy in your iCloud account and only vTasku on your devices can read it; we have no access. Delete all data also removes the iCloud copy.

Nothing else leaves your device. We do not use analytics or AI services on your data. We do not use the data of the app to track you across other apps and websites.

4. Ads and your choices

The Free plan shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may receive device identifiers, your IP address, ad interaction data and diagnostics. On iOS the ads are not personalized and vTasku does not ask to track you across apps. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland we ask for your consent first, and you can change your choice any time in Settings (Ad options). Plus users see no ads and the app does not send data to AdMob. See how Google uses information.

5. Purchases

Purchases are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play; we never see your card or payment details. To confirm your Plus access we use RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous user ID and your purchase history.

6. Sharing with the Vung app

If you turn it on, vTasku writes monthly totals per category to a file on your device so the Vung app, made by the same developer, can read them. The data does not travel over the network. Individual expenses, places, notes and receipt photos are not shared. You can turn this off and remove the shared file in Settings.

7. Invite codes and bonus Plus time

If you use invite codes, our server stores: a random user ID created on your device, your invite code, the end date of any bonus Plus time, and the public key and a counter of an Apple App Attest key. App Attest lets us check that requests come from a genuine copy of the app, and Apple takes part in this check. We do not store your name, email, IP address or expenses. When you use Delete all data in the app, your server record is deleted. A referral record may remain without any ID so that yearly limits keep working.

8. Hosting and logs

The server runs on Heroku in the European Union (Ireland). Heroku’s routing logs can contain IP addresses for a short time; our own logs contain only the request path, status code and duration. We use your IP address in memory only, to limit abuse.

9. How long we keep data

Data on your device stays until you delete it or remove the app. Server records stay until you use Delete all data or ask us to delete them. Third parties (Google, RevenueCat, Apple) keep data under their own policies.

10. Your rights

You can export your data (CSV) and delete everything in the app (Settings → Data and privacy). For data held by third parties or by our server you can ask for access, correction, deletion or objection under the law where you live (for example the GDPR in the EU/UK or Vietnam’s personal data protection law) by emailing tikki.dinh@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days. You may also complain to your data protection authority.

11. International transfers

Google and RevenueCat may process data in countries outside yours, including the United States. They rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses; see their policies.

12. Children

vTasku is for people aged 18 and over and does not knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

13. Planned: anonymous price contributions (not in this version)

A later version may offer an optional, off-by-default switch to contribute prices anonymously (public place name, category, amount and date, without time, location, notes, photos, IP address or user ID). It will show you exactly what is sent, can be turned off at any time, and we will update this policy and ask for your consent in the app before it starts.

14. Changes

For important changes we will tell you in the app before they apply. Effective date: 2026-11-15.
